# My agent: neighborhood-match
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps movers and families find the ideal neighborhood and home based on safety, school quality, and lifestyle needs with a catalog of neighborhoods and housing listings.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Family profile (budget, rent vs. buy, remote work needs, kid/school priorities, car/parking, safety preferences)
- Tools: Neighborhood stats lookup (crime rates, school ratings, transit/walk score) and housing listings search
- Catalog/UI: Comparison cards and tables of recommended neighborhoods and homes with pros/cons
- Image gen: Neighborhood vibe and streetscape concept images
- Sandbox: Affordability calculator (rent vs. buy mortgage, property tax, and commute cost breakdown)

Core rails (everyone): memory, tools, eval, deploy, frontend
My stretch menu (pick later): Catalog/UI (A2UI), Sandbox affordability calculator, Image gen
First eval question: "I'm moving with my family (one boy who needs good schools), have a remote job, and want a very safe neighborhood with low crime. Where should we look to rent an apartment under $3,000/month?"
